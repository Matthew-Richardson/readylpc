Ready, Set, Go! System

Ready

Be Ready, Stay Safe
  • Stay Informed — Sign up for LPC Alerts, enable Gov't Alerts, use Watch Duty
  • Make a Plan — People, Pets, Photos, Papers, and Pills
  • Fire Ready — Mitigation, "Go Kit" with protective clothing and masks
  • Know Your Routes — Don't hesitate on evacuation orders, know multiple ways out

Set

Pre-Evacuation

Voluntary evacuation. Evacuation orders are likely — be prepared to leave at a moment's notice.

Go

Evacuate Now

Leave immediately. Imminent threat to life. Area is closed to public access.
